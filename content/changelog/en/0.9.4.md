title: No more garbled terminal text on Windows
version: 0.9.4
date: 2026-11-03
---

An important fix for people using Persisty on Windows.

### Fixed

- **Session terminals no longer garble text on Windows.** With the display scaled to 125% or 150%, the terminal could end up with more columns than the session knew about: lines started on the right, leftovers of earlier screens stayed on screen, and scrolling made it worse. The terminal and the session now always share the same size.
