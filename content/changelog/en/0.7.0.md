Persisty now has a website in Portuguese, English and Spanish, with the plans, the download page and the Persisty Cloud account.

- **Account on the website**: sign in with a code sent by e-mail, the same as in the app. In My account you see your plan and the connected devices (and can revoke one), turn on the launch notice and record your interest in Enterprise.
- **Docs** with what the app does today and what it does not do yet.
- **Changelog** with an RSS feed in each language.
