Persisty now has an installer. On the Mac, the app is signed and notarised by Apple. On Windows and Linux it ships as a preview: unsigned and not yet tested on real machines. From now on, the app tells you when a new version is out and installs it in one click.

### Download and update

- The [download page](/download) offers the latest version for each system, and [All versions](/download/versions) lists every file with its date, size and SHA-256.
- The app looks for new versions when it opens and every 6 hours. The notice links to what's new and, when you ask, downloads, installs and restarts. You can turn the check off in **Settings › About**, and every check shows up in **Settings › Privacy & network**.
- On Windows and Linux, if there are open sessions, the app warns you before installing that they may be closed.

### Integrations

- **Settings › Integrations** with GitHub, GitLab (gitlab.com or your company's own) and Bitbucket, each with its own page and as many accounts as you like.
- **Add workspace** from a remote repository: paste a link from any service or pick from your accounts' list. The clone shows its progress and can be stopped.

### Git in the panel

- **↓** and **↑** arrows beside the branch show what is left to pull and push. The panel fetches, pulls, pushes and publishes the branch.
- Discard changes, commit everything when nothing is staged, amend and undo the last commit.
- Branches in the panel: switch, create, merge into the current one, rename and delete. Stash to put changes aside and bring them back.
- Conflicts and operations stopped half-way show how to go on or how to back out.

### Settings

- New tabs: **Appearance**, **About**, **Terminal** (font, cursor, display and keyboard), **Editor** (font, indentation, wrapping, line numbers and multiple cursors), **Files** (hide files by pattern in the tree, in search and in ⌘P), **Workspace** and **Notifications**.
- **Workspace**: save on its own after a pause, where sessions' own folders go, and a default IDE, with "Open in IDE" on the tab, in the palette and on the project.
- **Default agent**, preselected on ⌘⇧N and opened straight away on ⌘⌥N.

### Notifications

- When an agent waits for you or asks for permission, a notice appears in the app, with a sound, and a system notification.
- The **Notifications** tab has a test button and says where to look when the system notification does not show.

### After the computer restarts

- Claude Code comes back to the conversation it was in, in the same permission mode and in the session's own folder it worked in.
- A relaunched session no longer shows as ended by mistake.

### Projects and accounts

- **Archiving a project** asks what to do with its open sessions. Archived projects have a screen of their own, show up in ⌘K and can be deleted for good.
- Projects, session tabs and repositories stay in the order you drag them into. A Claude Code session's tab takes the conversation's name.
- **Account rotation (Pro)**: you choose at what share of the quota an account leaves the rotation (80%, 90%, 95% or only at the limit). When a Claude Code turn ends and the session's account has hit the limit, the session moves to the account with the most room and resumes the same conversation.
- Signing in to Persisty Cloud takes two steps, in a window of its own. A second account can sign in on the same computer, and a computer removed from the account comes back when you sign in on it again.

### Also

- ⌘ or Ctrl + click opens a terminal link in the browser.
- Create files and folders from the editor's file list.
- A mascot in the terminal's corner follows the agent's state.
