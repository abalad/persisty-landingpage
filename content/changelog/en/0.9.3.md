---
title: Sessions come back on their own after a restart
version: 0.9.3
date: 2026-11-03
---

After you restart your computer, sessions come back on their own when you open Persisty, and the restore report shows how it went.

### Fixed

- **Sessions come back on their own when you open the app.** With the default rule, **Relaunch if it went down** (and with **Always relaunch**), the sessions the restart took down are relaunched as soon as you open Persisty. Claude Code sessions come back in the same conversation. Before, they stayed at "Session has no process." until you clicked **Restore**. Sessions set to **Only reattach if alive** still wait for you to ask.
- **The restore report is back.** It opens on its own after that, and also after **Restore all** (<kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>⇧</kbd> <kbd>R</kbd>): what came back, what failed and why, session by session. The command palette opens the last one again.
