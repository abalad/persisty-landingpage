**Notes** is one Markdown notepad per project, for what you want at hand while you work: a plan, a list of what is left, commands you always forget. Every session of the project opens the same notes.

## Write notes

Open them from the **Notes** icon, in the tool bar on the session's right edge. The header shows **Notes ·** with the project's name and reminds you that they are Markdown, saved by themselves. The text has Markdown highlighting and the editor's font.

Notes are saved 0.6 seconds after you stop typing, and the header shows **Saving…** and then **Saved**. Closing the panel during that pause saves them too.

## Keys

Notes do not follow the editor's keymap: they use the standard text-editing keys. <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>S</kbd> saves at once, and <kbd>⌘</kbd> <kbd>F</kbd> searches within the notes. On Windows and Linux, which are in preview, use <kbd>Ctrl</kbd> instead of <kbd>⌘</kbd>.

## Plan and limits

Notes are on the free plan.

They live in Persisty's database on this computer, not in a file in the project's folder, so the agents do not read them. There is one notepad per project, and notes have no Markdown preview.
