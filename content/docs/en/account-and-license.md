The free plan needs no account. The Persisty Cloud account is for the paid plans and for the release e-mails you ask for: signing in with it in the app brings your plan's licence to this computer.

## Sign in

Without an account, the footer of **Settings** shows **Sign in** and **Create free account**. Both open the same window, in two steps. In the first, type your e-mail and click **Continue with e-mail**. In the second, type the 8-digit code that arrived by e-mail; with the last digit, the app signs you in on its own. The code works for 10 minutes, once. The account is created the first time you sign in, with no password.

If the code does not arrive, **Resend code** sends another after 60 seconds, and **Use another e-mail** goes back to the first step. If you already have a code, **Enter code** goes straight to the second step: asking for a new one would void the one that arrived.

The account's session is kept in the system's password vault: the Keychain on macOS and, in the previews, Credential Manager on Windows and Secret Service on Linux. Once you are signed in, the Settings footer shows the e-mail, the plan and **Manage account**, which opens **Settings › Account**. That tab only exists while you are signed in.

## Plan and features

**Settings › Account** shows the **E-mail**, the **Plan** (Free, Pro or Team), the **License** and the **Plan features**, each paid feature on a tag; on the free plan, **No paid features**. What each tag turns on is in [Plans and Pro features](/docs/pro).

On the free plan, the **Subscription** row has **See plans**, which opens the prices on the website. Below, **Refresh license** fetches the licence right away, and **Sign out** ends the account's session on this computer, which goes back to the free plan.

## Licence

Paid features come from a signed licence that the app checks on its own, offline. The **License** row shows **valid** and "Works offline until", with the date.

The licence lasts 14 days. While you are signed in and the app is open, Persisty asks for a new one when it opens and every 6 hours; offline, the stored one keeps working until it expires. If it expires or does not check out, the row shows **attention** and the reason, and the paid features stop until another one arrives: connect and click **Refresh license**.

## Computers

An account works on up to 5 computers. A computer joins the account's list the first time you sign in on it from the app, under the name it has on the system. On a sixth, you are signed in but no licence comes, and **Refresh license** says the account is already on 5 computers. Remove one in [My account](/account/devices) and refresh again.

A computer removed on the website leaves the account at the next licence renewal. To use it again, just sign in on it once more. **Sign out** does not free the place: the computer stays on the list until you remove it.

Once you sign out, another Persisty account can sign in on the same computer, which then also counts towards that account's 5.

## Account on the website

On the website, [Sign in](/sign-in) uses the same code by e-mail. In [My account](/account) you see the plan, manage the subscription through Stripe, invite your team, remove computers and turn on the e-mail that tells you when a new version is out.

## Plan and limits

Creating the account is free. An account covers up to 5 computers, and the licence lasts 14 days offline. If the system's password vault is not available, the account's session only lasts until you quit the app, and you have to sign in again when you reopen it.
