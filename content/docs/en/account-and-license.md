The free plan needs no account. The Persisty Cloud account exists for the paid plans and for the notices you ask for.

## Sign in

In the app, without an account, the footer of **Settings** shows **Sign in** and **Create free account**; both take the same path. You sign in with a code: enter your e-mail, receive a code and type it in the app or on the [website](/sign-in). The account is created the first time you sign in, with no password. The code works for 10 minutes, once.

In the app, the session is kept in the system's password vault: the Keychain on macOS, Credential Manager on Windows and Secret Service on Linux.

Once you are signed in, **Settings › Account** shows the e-mail, the **Plan**, the **License** ("Works offline until" and the date) and the **Plan features**. On the free plan, **See plans** opens the prices on the website. **Refresh license** fetches the licence now, and **Sign out** disconnects this computer from the account.

## Licence

Paid features are enabled by a signed licence that the app checks on its own.

- It is valid for **14 days without internet**. With the app open and a connection, it renews every 6 hours.
- It covers up to **5 computers** per account. In [My account](/account) you can see the devices and revoke one of them.
- If the plan ends, Persisty goes back to the free plan. Restore points already saved can still be restored, and requests already in the queue are still delivered. Rotation between accounts and the "continue" after the limit stop, since both need the licence.
