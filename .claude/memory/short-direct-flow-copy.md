On the cancelling dialog (2026-11-02) the user rejected the first version: a step with a paragraph plus a long list ("a pessoa que está cancelando não vai ler muito texto, precisamos ser mais assertivos e diretos"), a modal that scrolled, an offer step with a big empty area, and the mascot standing alone in an empty column. They approved: one title, one panel and at most a short list per step; prices and dates as figures (R$ 7,42/mês with the old price struck); checked lines instead of sentences; the mascot speaking one line per step in a bubble; faces matched to intent (puzzled when asked why, calm and content once the person got what they wanted, never sad at the end); the destructive action as a coral pill on the right.

**Why:** people in the middle of a decision scan, they don't read; empty space and scrolling read as unfinished.

**How to apply:** before showing any flow or dialog, cut every step to fit without scrolling on desktop, replace paragraphs with figures and short lines, and fill or remove empty areas. Show the texts with the before/after (see [[publishing-the-site]]).
